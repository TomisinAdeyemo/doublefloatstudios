ABOUT DOUBLE FLOAT STUDIOS

ADEYEMO OLUWATOMISIN

DF STUDIOS

In 2024, Adeyemo Oluwatomisin founded a forward-thinking creative agency designed to provide comprehensive solutions in web design, graphic design, and fashion design. Though still in its developmental stages, the agency is positioned to become a dynamic force in the creative industry, driven by a clear mission to blend functionality with artistic innovation. The agency distinguishes itself by offering a multi-disciplinary approach, ensuring that clients benefit from cohesive and impactful brand experiences across both digital and physical platforms. From modern, responsive websites to compelling visual identities and fashion concepts that merge creativity with culture, the firm seeks to deliver work that is both distinctive and future-focused. Under Oluwatomisin’s leadership, the agency aspires to grow into a trusted partner for businesses, brands, and individuals, while building a reputation for creativity, professionalism, and forward-looking design solutions. As it develops, it continues to embody the founder’s vision of a hub where art, technology, and style converge to shape the next era of creative expression.
